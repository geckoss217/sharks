    • Spend, impressions, clicks, Google conversions, Quality Score and search terms come from the main Google Ads account (299-875-2313) through the Google Ads API. The second Google account and Microsoft Ads aren't connected for spend yet, so their spend columns show “—”.
    • Leads, quotable leads, booked jobs and revenue come from WhatConverts paid search leads (google or bing, cpc), for every account. Transactions and appointments aren't counted as leads. WhatConverts has no leads before Apr 1, 2025 and no transactions before Sept 25, 2025, so leads and jobs are blank before those dates.
    • A booked job gets the keyword WhatConverts stored on the job. When the job has none, it takes the keyword of the most recent paid lead from the same phone or email. Jobs with neither land on “(no keyword)” under their campaign. Jobs count in the week they were booked; cancelled jobs are excluded.
    • About 40% of Google paid leads arrive in WhatConverts with no keyword and no campaign. They show as “(no keyword)” so the totals still match the Ads Scorecard funnel.
    • Leads join to keywords on campaign + keyword text, so a keyword running as both Exact and Phrase in one campaign is one row (match types shown as chips). Group by Keyword to combine a keyword across campaigns and platforms.
    • Search terms list only terms that got at least one click. “Add as keyword” = the term converted in Google Ads and isn't a keyword yet. “Negative?” = $100+ spend, no Google conversions, and the term isn't itself one of your keywords. “Already negative” = the exact term is on a negative list for that campaign. Both are prompts to review, not changes. Log any change in the Ads Change Log first.
    • Flags: “Spend, no leads” = $250+ spend and 0 leads. “Leads, no jobs” = 8+ leads and 0 booked jobs (only for ranges after Sept 22, 2025). “Low QS” = Quality Score 4 or lower. “Hold” = the campaign has an active hold in the Ads Change Log.
    • Data refreshes every Monday morning. Column layout and view choices are saved in this browser only.
