Best time slots

    By day of week

    • Leads: one per inquiry. Calls, forms and chats from the same phone or email within 30 days count once, at the time of the first contact. Test and internal calls (Sales Stage "Marketing-Sales Call") are left out.
    • Quotable leads: inquiries where any contact was marked Quotable = Yes.
    • Bookings: Transaction items in WhatConverts, placed at the time the Transaction was created. Cancelled transactions are left out. Transactions start in September 2025, so earlier periods show no bookings.
    • Close rate: booked inquiries ÷ quotable inquiries, placed at the time the lead came in, so you can see which hours bring in leads that turn into jobs. Squares with fewer than quotable leads are hatched because the rate isn't reliable yet.
